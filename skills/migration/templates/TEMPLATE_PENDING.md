# Pending template

Start with Change, Targets, and Verification. Include Constraints and Open questions only when relevant. Add Execution during preparation and Progress once execution starts. Record unknowns under Open questions.

```markdown
# <Migration title>

## Change
<Current state, intended state, and reason for the change.>

## Targets
- <Environment and affected data or resources.>

## Constraints
<Relevant constraints, dependencies, and ordering relative to deployment.>

## Verification
- <Check and observable result required for success.>

## Open questions
- <Unresolved information or decision that affects the migration.>

## Execution
- Plan: <Prepared operations and order.>
- Preflight: <Checks performed, results, and remaining uncertainty.>
- Stop conditions: <Conditions that require stopping.>
- Recovery: <Safe retry, resumption, or reversal; required resources and readiness.>

## Progress
- <Timestamp and target: operations performed, results, and verification evidence. Include failures, remaining work, and information needed to resume safely.>
```
